RAP ROADMAP

Zero level se Professional Rapper tak ka full process.

1. The Art of Rap (Phase-wise)

Writing & Lyrics

  • Rhyme Schemes: AABB, ABAB aur Multi-syllabic rhymes seekho.
  • Wordplay: Metaphors, Similes aur Punchlines par focus karo.
  • Storytelling: Apne real-life experiences ko verse mein badlo.

Flow & Rhythm

  • The Count: 4/4 beat par bars likhna seekho.
  • Cadence: Alag-alag tarike se word pronounce karke rhythm banao.
  • Speed: Pehle slow beats par control pao, phir fast flow try karo.

Delivery & Voice

  • Breath Control: Verse ke beech mein kahan saans leni hai, ye fix karo.
  • Confidence: Voice mein energy aur bhari-pan (chest voice) lao.
  • Emotions: Sad song aur Aggressive song ki tone alag rakho.

Recording & Mixing

  • Layering: Main vocals ke sath ad-libs aur backing vocals record karo.
  • EQ & Compression: Awaz ko clear aur loud banane ke liye basics seekho.
  • Distro: Spotify aur YouTube par apna music release karna seekho.
