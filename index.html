RAP ROADMAP

Zero se Hero tak ka safar, hip-hop ki duniya mein.

01

Suno aur Samjho (Deep Listening)

Sirf gaane mat suno, beats aur lyrics ke structure ko observe karo. Old school se lekar modern trap tak sab explore karo.

02

Vocabulary & Rhyme Book

Ek diary banao. Roz naye words aur rhyming pairs likho. Metaphors aur similes ka use seekho.

03

Master the Flow

Beat (Instrumental) par bina ruke bolne ki practice karo. Counting beats (1, 2, 3, 4) seekho taaki tum off-beat na jao.

04

Write Your First Verse

4 ya 8 lines se shuru karo. Topic wo chuno jo tumhare dil ke paas ho. Authenticity is key!

05

Record & Analyze

Phone ke mic par record karo aur khud suno. Dekho kahan improve kar sakte ho, phir dobara try karo.
